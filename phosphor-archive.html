01

Arcade Space Combat

Fly through compact combat sectors, react to enemy pressure, and complete distinct mission objectives while the renderer builds the game's CRT-like identity from line work, glow, bloom, and phosphor persistence.

△

Vector Identity

Ships, hazards, projectiles, and environments are presented with a clean line-based visual language and multi-pass phosphor rendering.

◎

Campaign Sectors

Missions introduce objectives such as asteroid destruction, satellite hacking, and planetary assaults rather than repeating one endless encounter.

∞

Endless Mode

A procedural survival stream extends the combat loop beyond the authored campaign and pushes upgrades, enemy density, and endurance.

⌁

Responsive Control

The Android version is designed around direct touch movement, with gamepad support adapted toward the same readable handling model.

02

Current Status

Android release in open testing

The Android repository is the production source of truth. A separate desktop port is being prepared for itch.io and possible later Steam distribution.
