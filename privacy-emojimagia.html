Privacy Policy

Application: Emoji Magia Package: com.mothblank.emojimagia Last Updated: October 2, 2026

MOTHblank ("we", "us", or "our") develops and operates Emoji Magia, an Android game. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the game, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Emoji Magia is designed so that ordinary gameplay progress is primarily stored on your device. Network features are used for advertising, Google Play services, purchases, consent management, and—when enabled—account-backed economy synchronization.

1. Information Processed by Emoji Magia

A. Age / audience classification

The game may ask you to enter a date of birth so it can apply an age-appropriate audience profile before advertising services are initialized. The exact birth date is used transiently on the device and is not retained. The game stores only a derived local classification such as restricted minor or adult.

B. Local game data

Gameplay progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device. This can include settings, progression, unlocks, run history, currency balances, Diamond Shop ownership, selected gameplay options, and custom-hero configuration.

Android may back up application data according to your device and Google account backup settings.

C. Local and first-party telemetry

Emoji Magia records limited monetization and store interaction counters for diagnostics, such as whether an offer was shown, selected, completed, or failed. These counters are stored locally.

When the cloud economy is configured, authenticated, and the audience profile is adult, a limited subset of monetization events may also be sent to MOTHblank's first-party backend. That event payload is designed not to include an advertising ID, device ID, birth date, Play Games player ID, or gameplay run/scope identifier.

D. Cloud economy and Play Games

If account-backed economy synchronization is enabled and you connect Google Play Games, Emoji Magia may process an account identifier and synchronize economy-related state. This can include diamond balances, earned/purchased diamond totals, owned shop items, selected pact, custom-hero configuration, Premium ownership, and synchronization revision data.

Purchase tokens and product identifiers may be sent to the first-party backend when needed to verify Google Play purchases and prevent duplicate or fraudulent grants.

2. Advertising and Consent

Emoji Magia uses Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy and consent handling where applicable.

  • Advertising is not initialized until the game has resolved the local audience classification.
  • For restricted minors, the app configures child/restricted treatment, limits ad content to Google's G-rated inventory setting, and disables publisher ad personalization before initializing the advertising SDK.
  • For adults, UMP may request or manage advertising consent based on applicable requirements and exposes privacy options when required.
  • Rewarded ads are user-initiated; gameplay rewards are granted only after the ad SDK reports that the reward was earned.

Google's advertising services may process device, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, and other information according to Google's own policies and your consent/settings.

3. Purchases and Google Play

Emoji Magia uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases such as Premium or paid currency when those products are enabled. Google processes the payment. Emoji Magia receives the technical purchase information necessary to determine product ownership, acknowledge or consume purchases, and—when cloud verification is enabled—verify entitlements with the first-party backend.

We do not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.

4. Third-Party Services

The game integrates Google services whose own privacy terms also apply:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Google Play data and privacy information

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by Emoji Magia is used to operate the game, save progress, apply age-appropriate advertising behavior, deliver rewarded-ad benefits, manage consent choices, process and verify purchases, synchronize eligible account-backed economy data, prevent duplicate grants or abuse, and diagnose monetization/store behavior.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Children's and Teen Privacy

Emoji Magia may be used by younger users. The game therefore resolves an audience classification before initializing advertising. The exact entered birth date is not stored. Restricted-minor profiles receive stricter advertising configuration and do not participate in the app's first-party remote monetization telemetry described above.

Google Play, Family Link, device settings, and applicable law may impose additional controls on purchases and account features.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on the device until it is erased through available in-app controls, Android application-data controls, or uninstallation, subject to Android/Google backup behavior.

If first-party cloud economy data has been created, you may request deletion of that account-linked data by contacting MOTHblank at the address below. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

Data held directly by Google is controlled by Google's retention and account-management policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect first-party data, including authenticated account flows and HTTPS for production backend communication. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Emoji Magia changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the date above. You should review this page periodically, especially when new online or monetization features are introduced.
