Privacy Policy

Application: NotaSeguraPackage: com.mothblank.notaseguraLast Updated: October 3, 2026

MOTHblank ("we", "us", or "our") develops and operates NotaSegura, an Android application for organizing purchase records, receipts, warranties, and payment reminders.

NotaSegura is designed as a local-first application. The app itself does not request Android's Internet permission and does not include advertising, MOTHblank analytics, user accounts, or cloud synchronization. It uses Google ML Kit through Google Play services for on-device OCR; related operational telemetry is described below.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

NotaSegura can store information you enter about purchases and payments, including product names, merchants, purchase values and dates, warranty dates, categories, model and serial numbers, notes, payment schedules, payment status, and related metadata.

This structured information is stored in a local Room database in the app's private storage.

2. Receipt and Document Attachments

You can attach images and PDF documents such as receipts, invoices, warranty documents, and other proof-of-purchase material. Imported files are copied into app-private storage and associated with the corresponding purchase record.

These documents may themselves contain personal or sensitive information. NotaSegura does not upload them to MOTHblank or a cloud service.

3. On-Device OCR

For supported images, NotaSegura uses on-device text recognition to extract receipt text and generate reviewable suggestions such as merchant, purchase date, total value, model number, and serial number.

Recognized text can be stored locally with the attachment so that records can be searched later. OCR output is not sent to MOTHblank.

4. Notifications and Reminders

NotaSegura can use Android notifications for payment and purchase-related reminders. On Android versions that require runtime notification permission, the app asks for that permission after explaining why it is useful.

Reminder scheduling and notification state are handled locally on the device.

5. Backups, Restore, and Exports

NotaSegura can create a portable .notasegura backup containing your structured records and managed documents. Portable backups include integrity validation so corrupted or incomplete archives can be rejected during restore. NotaSegura does not encrypt exported backup archives, so they should be stored only in locations you trust.

You control where exported backup files are stored and shared. Once a backup leaves the app's private storage, you are responsible for safeguarding and deleting that exported copy when it is no longer needed.

The app can also create human-readable PDF reports for sharing. Shared reports are created as temporary files and exposed to the receiving app through Android's controlled file-sharing mechanism.

6. Network Access and Third Parties

NotaSegura does not request Android's Internet permission. The application does not contain an advertising SDK, MOTHblank analytics SDK, MOTHblank account service, or cloud database.

For receipt OCR, NotaSegura uses Google ML Kit text recognition through Google Play services. Receipt and document contents and recognized text are processed on-device and are not uploaded to MOTHblank. According to Google's ML Kit data disclosure, the Android SDK may collect device and application information, device or installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, feature input/output size, event types, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this telemetry is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Distribution, purchases, installation, updates, crash handling performed by the operating system or store, and other Google Play platform activity are governed by Google Play and your Google account settings rather than by NotaSegura's own data layer.

7. Android Backup

Automatic Android application backup and device-to-device transfer of NotaSegura's private app data are disabled by the application's backup and data-extraction configuration. Portable backups are created only when you explicitly choose to export one.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local NotaSegura data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Exported PDFs and backup archives remain wherever you chose to save or share them until you delete those copies yourself.

9. Security

Structured records and managed documents are stored in app-private Android storage. Exported backup archives include integrity checks but are not encrypted by NotaSegura, so users should protect the destination where those files are saved. No security mechanism can guarantee absolute protection.

10. Children's Privacy

NotaSegura does not require an account, advertising profile, date of birth, or online identity. The app is intended as a general-purpose personal organization utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a MOTHblank server.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when NotaSegura or its data-handling features change. The date above identifies the current version.
