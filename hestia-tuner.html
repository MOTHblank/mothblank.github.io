Tune and Keep Time

Hestia combines a dedicated pitch-detection interface with a mechanical-style metronome, keeping both tools available without turning the app into a larger music workstation.

Precision Tuner

Optimized McLeod Pitch Method analysis identifies the note, octave, frequency, and cents offset for responsive instrument tuning.

Stable Needle

Pitch smoothing and deliberate display motion prioritize a readable, holdable tuning target instead of noisy frame-by-frame movement.

Mechanical Metronome

Adjust tempo from 40 to 300 BPM, choose time signatures, and hear a distinct accented downbeat.

Background Timing

A foreground service and low-latency Android audio APIs keep metronome playback consistent while the app remains active.
