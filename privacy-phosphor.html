Privacy Policy

Application: Phosphor Archive Last Updated: July 3, 2026

MOTHblank ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application Phosphor Archive (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information directly from you. However, to provide and improve our game, the Service integrates third-party tools that may collect user data.

A. Local Game Save Data

All game progress, campaign unlocks, settings, preferences, and meta upgrades are stored locally on your device. This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

B. Third-Party Services

The Service uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

2. AdMob Advertising and Identifiers

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the game (such as rewarded video ads to revive the player or purchase shop upgrades). Google AdMob uses device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

You can manage your personalized advertising settings directly in your Android device's settings menu (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization or Reset advertising ID).

3. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal data to a third-party service through our app, please contact the third-party service provider or contact us so we can take necessary actions.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
