NotaSegura

01

Keep the proof, not the clutter

Each purchase can keep the information that matters later: merchant, value, purchase date, warranty, category, model, serial number, notes, and attached receipt or invoice documents.

▤

Receipt archive

Store purchases with image or PDF attachments in app-private storage instead of relying on fragile paper or scattered gallery files.

⌕

Local OCR

On-device text recognition extracts reviewable suggestions such as merchant, purchase date, total, model, and serial number.

◷

Useful reminders

Track pending payments and warranty dates, with Android notifications for obligations that deserve attention.

⇄

Integrity-checked backup

Create and restore versioned .notasegura archives containing structured records and original managed documents, with integrity validation during restore.

02

Local-first by design

NotaSegura has no Internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics service, app account, or cloud sync. Its Room database, OCR text, and managed documents remain on the device unless you explicitly export a report or backup.

03

Current status

Preparing for Google Play closed testing

The current focus is release hardening, persistence, backup/restore reliability, accessibility, and validation before public distribution.
