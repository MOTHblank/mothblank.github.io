Privacy Policy

Application: Hestia Tuner (Afinador Hestia) Last Updated: July 3, 2026

MOTHblank ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application Hestia Tuner (Afinador Hestia) (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Microphone Permission & Audio Processing

To provide high-precision pitch detection and tuning capabilities, the Service requires access to your device's microphone (via the android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO permission).

Critical Privacy Commitment:

  • All audio signals captured by the microphone are processed in real-time on your device using local mathematical pitch-detection algorithms.
  • Audio data is **never recorded**, stored, saved, or uploaded to any remote server or third party.
  • Microphone access is active only while using the tuning feature and is immediately released when the feature is not in use.

2. Background Execution & Services

The Service utilizes an Android Foreground Service to run the metronome engine. This ensures precise timing and audio execution without interruption when the app is in the background or the screen is turned off. This service does not collect, track, or share any personal information.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

We do not collect or share personal information directly. However, the Service integrates third-party tools that may collect user data. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Google AdMob uses device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. You can manage or reset these settings on your Android device under Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided data via third-party integrations, please contact the third-party providers or reach out to us.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
